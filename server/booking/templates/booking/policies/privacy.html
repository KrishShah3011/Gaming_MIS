What we store

Your name and email address from Google, the mobile number you give us, your bookings and your wallet history. Payments are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store your card or bank details.

How we use it

To show and manage your bookings, keep your wallet balance correct, and contact you about a booking. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't send marketing messages.

Café staff, to run the café. Other customers only see whether a PC is free or taken, never who booked it.

Your choices

To correct your details or delete your account, contact us. Wallet history is kept as long as the law requires for payment records.
