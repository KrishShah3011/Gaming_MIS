Cancellation and refunds

Cancelling a booking

You can cancel each booked PC separately from My bookings, up to {{ cafe.cancel_cutoff_min }} minutes before it starts. The full amount you paid for that PC, including any time you added, goes straight back to your wallet.

After the cutoff

Bookings can't be cancelled after the cutoff, once they've started, or if you don't turn up. There's no refund for these.

If something goes wrong on our side

If a PC breaks or the café has to close, staff will cancel your booking and refund it to your wallet in full.

Wallet top-ups

Refunds go to your wallet, not back to your bank or card. If you need your remaining balance back, contact us and the café will refund it in cash.
